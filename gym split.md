### Treadmill

Since your current goal is mainly:

- Build gym consistency
- Gain muscle in upper body
- Not prioritize endurance

I would do:

### Before lifting

**5–10 minutes treadmill walk**

- Incline: 3–8%
- Speed: comfortable brisk walk

Purpose:

- Raise body temperature
- Increase blood flow
- Get joints moving

Do **not** run hard before lifting. You'll just make yourself tired.

---

### After lifting (optional)

**10–20 minutes incline walking**

- Speed: 4–6 km/h
- Incline: 5–12%

Benefits:

- Burns extra calories
- Improves cardiovascular fitness
- Doesn't interfere much with recovery

If you're trying to gain weight and muscle, keep it around 10 minutes.

If you're trying to lose fat, 15–20 minutes is fine.

---

# Before Exercise: Dynamic Warm-up

Avoid long static stretches before lifting.

Instead do 5 minutes of dynamic movements:

### Upper Body Days

#### Arm Circles

- 15 forward
- 15 backward

#### Shoulder Rolls

- 10 each direction

#### Band Pull Aparts

(if available)

- 15 reps

#### Arm Swings

- 15 reps

#### Scapular Retractions

- 10 reps

#### Warm-up Sets

Before your first heavy exercise:

Example:

Bench Press working weight = 20kg dumbbells

Do:

- 10 reps with very light weight
- 8 reps with moderate weight
- Then start working sets

This is more important than stretching.

---

### Leg Day

#### Bodyweight Squats

- 15 reps

#### Leg Swings

- 10 each leg front/back

#### Walking Lunges

- 10 steps

#### Hip Circles

- 10 each direction

Then warm-up sets on the first leg exercise.

---

# What About Stretching?

### Before Workout

Avoid holding stretches for 30–60 seconds.

Research generally shows long static stretching immediately before heavy lifting can slightly reduce force production.

---

### After Workout

You can stretch:

- Chest stretch
- Triceps stretch
- Lat stretch
- Forearm stretch

Hold each for 20–30 seconds.

This won't magically prevent soreness, but many people find it improves flexibility and feels good.

---

A practical gym session would look like:

1. 5–10 min treadmill walk
2. 3–5 min dynamic warm-up
3. Warm-up sets for first exercise
4. Main workout
5. 10–15 min incline walk (optional)
6. Quick stretching (optional)

That entire routine is enough for a beginner without turning a 60-minute workout into a 2-hour session.
